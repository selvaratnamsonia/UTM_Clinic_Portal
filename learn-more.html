About UTM Clinic Portal

Providing accessible healthcare for the UTM community.

Our Mission

The UTM Clinic Portal is designed to streamline healthcare services for students and staff. Our goal is to reduce waiting times and provide a digital-first approach to medical appointments and records.

Online Booking

Skip the queue by scheduling your visit in advance. Choose your preferred doctor and time slot with just a few clicks.

Digital MC

Access your Medical Certificates and consultation history directly from your dashboard anytime, anywhere.

Emergency Info

Find quick contact details for UTM emergency response and local hospitals in Skudai and Batu Pahat.
